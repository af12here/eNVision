Our Origin

Our Story

eNVision was founded in 2018 with a vision to redefine modern eyewear through minimalist design, premium quality, and everyday comfort. What began as a small atelier in Jakarta has grown into a destination for individuals who value both clarity and confidence.

What started as a passion for timeless optical fashion has grown into a global brand attracting customers from around the world — united by a shared appreciation for design that serves purpose as much as beauty.

Today, eNVision continues to pursue its mission: making premium eyewear accessible, personal, and above all — extraordinary.

Meet Our Founders

Founder

Alexander Ferdian

Founder & Creative Director

Alexander founded eNVision with a passion for combining modern aesthetics, premium craftsmanship, and everyday functionality into a single eyewear experience. Inspired by minimalist fashion and contemporary optical design, he believes that eyewear should reflect confidence, personality, and lifestyle.
